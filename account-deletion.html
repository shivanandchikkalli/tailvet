Delete your TailVet account

You can request account deletion in the app or by email.

Delete from the app

  1. Sign in to TailVet with the Google account associated with your profile.
  2. Open your Profile tab and choose Delete account.
  3. Review the deletion notice and confirm. You may be asked to sign in again to verify your identity.

Deletion removes your account profile, pet profiles, reminders, standalone symptom checks and photos, and consultation chat messages. Your vet profile is also removed if you are a veterinarian.

Delete some data without deleting your account

  • Delete a pet profile: open the Pets tab, select the pet, tap the delete icon, and confirm.
  • Delete a reminder: open the Reminders tab and swipe the reminder left.

Deleting a pet does not automatically remove related symptom-check or appointment records. To request deletion of specific symptom-check, photo, chat, or other account data without deleting your account, email us using the instructions below. Appointment and prescription audit records may be de-identified and retained as described in our Privacy Policy.

Request deletion by email

Email us from the Google account registered with TailVet. Include “TailVet data deletion request” in the subject, identify whether you want to delete your account or specific data, and tell us the account email address. Do not send passwords or payment information. If you cannot access the app, you can request full account deletion by email.
